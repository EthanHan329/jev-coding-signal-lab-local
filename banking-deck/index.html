Probability needs validation before automation

Conceptual illustration of calibration, not a test result or guarantee for one case.

Guo et al., On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks, ICML 201717 / 27
Presenter notes
Timing: 1.5 minutes. Calibration is an aggregate statistical property, not a guarantee for an individual case. A high confidence score cannot establish authorisation or eliminate correlated errors. Guo et al. show that modern neural networks can be poorly calibrated and evaluate temperature scaling. Post-hoc calibration must use held-out data, with final evaluation on a separate test set. A binary probability and a vendor confidence field need not have the same semantics. Supporting detail: Among comparable cases scored near 0.8, about 80% should satisfy the predicted event. Bank validation Measure calibration on untouched, representative cases. Check language, product and customer segments. Measure error versus automation coverage. Recheck after model or population changes.

Appendix: The data advantage comes from adjudicated decisions

Training evidence

  • Historical cases with authorised data use.
  • Clear label definitions and policy versions.
  • Two reviewers for ambiguous or high-risk cases.
  • Explicit disagreements and “insufficient evidence”.

Evaluation protection

  • Split by time and customer or case family.
  • Keep duplicates and related cases together.
  • Hold out rare intents and difficult language.
  • Reserve a locked test set for release decisions.
Agentic banking · introductory session24 / 27
Presenter notes
Optional appendix; outside the 40-minute running order. Do not treat past staff actions as perfect ground truth. They can encode inconsistent policy and unequal treatment. Dataset size follows class frequency, learning curves and the precision needed for the release claim.
